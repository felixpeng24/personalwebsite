Claremont Accelerator

Building the Largest Student-Run Accelerator at the 5Cs

Timeline: Fall 2024–Present Role: Intern → VP → Co-President Location: Claremont, CA

Overview

Co-led the largest student-run accelerator at the Claremont Colleges, growing cohorts from 4 startups (Fall 2024) to 6 startups (Spring 2025, Fall 2025), each supported by ~4 student interns. Raised $84,000+ from alumni, RLCIE, The Hive, Cooley, and corporate sponsors (Google Cloud, AWS), funding ~$6,000 equity-free grants per startup plus $40,000+ in perks.

Built partnerships with 35+ alumni mentors and hosted 20+ speaker events averaging 100+ attendees, including trustees and program directors. Drove measurable startup outcomes: Glam Up (100k → 1M+ downloads, top 3 App Store), ArtHub (mockup → live product), Stratos Vanguard (2x efficiency gains), and Brain (pilot with 200 students).

Leadership Journey

Intern (Fall 2024)

Joined as one of the first interns. Built a Retrieval-Augmented Generation chatbot prototype using Microsoft Azure Document Intelligence to support financial institutions, cutting hedge fund research prep by ~50%.

Vice President (Spring 2025)

Led fundraising that secured $84,000 from alumni and institutional partners (majority from RLCIE, alongside The Hive, Cooley, and corporate sponsors). Recruited startups, wrote grant applications, matched interns with founders, and expanded alumni mentor engagement.

Co-President (Fall 2025)

Overseeing all operations including recruitment, partnerships, finances, and Demo Day. Leading Cohort 3 expansion and long-term strategic planning for the accelerator's growth across the Claremont Colleges.

Startup Success Stories

Glam Up

Scaled from 100k+ to 1M+ downloads, ranked top 3 on App Store (beating Hinge and Tinder in October 2024). Demonstrated the accelerator's ability to support consumer app scaling.

ArtHub

Progressed from mockup to fully functional product; interns integrated into the core team. Shows how the intern-matching program creates lasting value for startups.

Stratos Vanguard

Doubled products (2 → 4) and improved efficiency 2x using AWS-powered algorithms, showcasing technical infrastructure support from corporate partnerships.

Brain

Advanced from concept to live pilot with 200 students, validating product-market fit through accelerator mentorship and resources.

Strategic Partnerships

Established formal partnerships with The Hive, RLCIE (Rose Lee Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship), Cooley (legal services), AWS, and Google Cloud. These partnerships provide startups with:

  • $40,000+ in cloud computing credits and perks
  • Pro bono legal services through Cooley
  • Institutional funding and credibility through RLCIE and The Hive
  • Access to 35+ alumni mentors from successful startups and tech companies
  • Speaker events with 100+ attendees including trustees and program directors
